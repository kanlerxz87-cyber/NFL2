Suggestion review console

A place to review every suggestion and choose a path before anything is implemented. Nothing on this page has been built on your instruction: it orders the 45 suggestions the register already holds, scores them on the fields the register already publishes, and bundles them into four candidate paths so you can approve a path rather than forty-five separate items.

How the score is produced — and what it is not

priority_score = impact_pts / (effort_pts + risk_pts), where the register's own codes become H=3, M=2, L=1 for impact and L=1, M=2, H=3 for effort, and the risk text is reduced to its first word (Low=1, Medium=2, High=3).

This is arithmetic on published fields, not evidence. It orders the register; it does not rank the world. Two suggestions scoring 1.50 are tied because their fields are tied, not because they are worth the same. The point of publishing the arithmetic is that you can change the weights and see the order move — the inputs are all on the suggestion register.

What to decide, in the order I would take them

  1. Pick a path, not a list. Path A (prove the data layer) is a prerequisite for any of the others being believable, and it is the cheapest — 9 of its 16 items are quick wins. Path B (prices) is the one that unlocks 9 of the 14 validation gates and the only one you have to pay for.
  2. Answer Q-01 — the price feed. Until that is settled, Path C (the availability signal this session just extended to 31 clubs) cannot be validated, only built. That is a defensible reason to do A first and B second.
  3. Decide the refresh cadence (Q-40, S-43). The standings were one game stale on 13 November and nothing on the site recorded when they were read (FL-35).
  4. Decide whether the week 1–9 game log stays frozen (Q-41, S-26). It is the pre-registered corpus; appending to it invalidates every grade.

    5. What this console deliberately does not do

    • It does not save your choices. Every published page on this site is generated and has no
    • JavaScript, so a triage tick-box would be lost on the next build. The durable place for a
    • decision is the decision column of data/suggestions.csv, and the change is one line.
    • It does not weight impact more cleverly than the register does. Any weighting you prefer is a
    • change to one dict in scripts/build_s745_decisions.py.
    • It does not score the 40 flagged irregularities. Those are defects and gaps, not
    • proposals; they are on Flagged for Review.
