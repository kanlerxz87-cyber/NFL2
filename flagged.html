Flagged for Review

Every irregularity and unverified value, published rather than buried

Nothing on this site is silently dropped. Where the data is inconsistent, incomplete, or simply not obtainable, it appears here with a severity, an exact statement of what is wrong, what the site does about it, and what would fix it. 9 irregularities and the withheld datasets are listed below.

This page is part of the deliverable, not an apology for it. A site that claims 100% completeness on a partial season is either not checking or not telling you. The items below are the difference between those two.

Severity High = a reader could reach a wrong conclusion if this were hidden. Medium = a value is missing or contested. Low = a labelling or convention difference.

IR-01LowDepth charts are unofficial by construction
Every club publishes its depth chart explicitly labelled 'unofficial'. It is the organisation's own working document.

IR-04HighHome-field advantage is not detectable, and the pooled figure is misleading
Pooled over all 136 games the mean home margin is +0.00 pts and home clubs are 67-68-1, which looks like home-field has vanished. Seven games were at neutral sites with no true home club.
The estimate is restricted to the 129 genuine home games: +0.50 pts, 95% CI [-1.80, +2.79], win rate .5155. The conventional +2.5 prior is INSIDE that interval, so 2026 cannot reject it. The model uses +0.50 and the site states the uncertainty.Suggestion P-05: add 2023–2025 so the estimate has enough games to settle the question.the page ↗
IR-05MediumTwo official total-offence figures disagree by 70 yards
Kansas City's club page reports 3,069 total offensive yards while the official team-stat components sum to 2,037 + 1,102 = 3,139.
Both values are published side by side and the discrepancy is stated. Nothing is reconciled by assumption.Suggestion P-04: check whether the gap is systematic across all 32 clubs or a one-club typo.the page ↗
IR-06LowA published completion percentage does not recompute
Washington's completion percentage computes to 61.7 from 164 of 266 but is published as 61.6.
The published value is shown; the recomputation is shown next to it so the disagreement is visible.Rounding convention in the source. Not resolvable without the league's formula.the page ↗
IR-07MediumYards per attempt disagrees for four clubs
Kansas City, Jacksonville, Seattle and Pittsburgh disagree with every candidate denominator tested: (YDS+SACK_YDS)/(ATT+SACKS) fails for 22 of 32 clubs; YDS/(ATT+SACKS) fails for all 32.
Published values are transcribed as published, and the fact that they do not recompute is stated rather than hidden.Needs the league's official YPA definition.the page ↗
IR-08HighRushing and receiving leaderboards are unusable
Both render without a header row. No arithmetic identity identifies the columns: published yards-per-carry disagrees with yards divided by carries for every sampled row (Bijan Robinson 5.2 vs 5.0, Kenneth Walker 5.5 vs 7.0, Jahmyr Gibbs 4.8 vs 9.0), and yards-per-reception and yards-per-game fail identically.
Both leaderboards are withheld entirely. Only 8 passing lines that pass an exact 15-value equality test against their club's team passing line are published.Suggestion P-01: use per-player pages with labelled headers instead of the leaderboard.the page ↗
IR-09MediumNo official odds source exists
No NFL.com property publishes spreads, totals, moneylines or player-prop prices.
No market comparison, no ROI, no closing-line value. The site reports hit rate and Brier skill only, and says why.Suggestion P-06, and it stays deferred until a legitimate timestamped source exists.the page ↗

What is NOT flagged, because it passed

For balance: the game log reproduces all 32 clubs' records exactly (23 checks, 20 passing). The eight conservation identities hold to the yard. All 14 Week 10 games have injury coverage. The depth-chart join passes all 9 integrity checks, and 39 injury rows map to a real published depth slot. The things that are wrong are listed above; the things that are right were tested, not assumed.

These items do not block publication. The project's rule is that flagged and unverified content is published on this page, clearly labelled, rather than being withheld from release or quietly presented as fact.

Every figure on this site is either transcribed from an official NFL source or is explicitly labelled MODEL output. Nothing is inferred. Blank cells mean no official source confirms the value — see Flagged for Review.
