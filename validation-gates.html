Status, stated plainly

Zero strategies can currently pass any gate, and the matrix on the leaderboard page shows why: the inputs for the earliest gates — a licensed price feed, a full roster join, a point-in-time store — do not exist in this repository. That is not a criticism of the specifications; it is the reason no number on this site is called an edge.
