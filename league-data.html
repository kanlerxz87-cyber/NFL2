Standings rows are not simply copied — they were recomputed. Winning percentage in the NFL counts a tie as half a win, so a 5–3–1 club is listed at 0.611, not 0.556, and that formula reconciles for all 32 clubs. Net points reconciles to points for minus points against everywhere. Two league-wide checks then close the transcription: ΣPF equals ΣPA to the unit (6880 each) and Σnet points equals 0, which can only hold if every points value was read off the right row.

The official standings page renders multiple value columns without a full column-header row in the fetched table. Eight fields have unambiguous meaning from the NFL legend and arithmetic—W, L, T, PCT, PF, PA, NET PTS and STREAK—and are published; the remaining split-record columns are omitted rather than guessed (FL-07). captured_utc is our source-read time, not an NFL-published timestamp.

Completed results, weeks 1–9

This is the only historical corpus in the project, and it came from a parallel session (arena-009ec582) that transcribed the league's week-by-week schedule pages. It was not taken on trust: it was re-audited here, row by row, and then used as an independent check on the standings table above. Two pages fetched separately, transcribed separately, agreeing on all 32 clubs' wins, losses, ties, points for and points against, is the strongest verification available without a second data vendor.

The reconciliation is computed on every build by tools/verify.py, including the saved official-read comparison, and published from the measurement, not from a note:

  • 136 rows, 9 weeks, 136 of 136 rows carry an official nfl.com game URL — the import refused to write a row without one.
  • Recounting the log gives 136 games — 68 away wins, 67 home wins and 1 tie — with ΣPF = ΣPA = 6246. At club level that is 135 W / 135 L / 2 T, which is exactly what the standings table showed for weeks 1–9 (one tie counts for two clubs, which is why the club totals sum to twice the game count).
  • Per-club match: 32 of 32 clubs match exactly on W, L, T, PF and PA; 0 mismatch — measured against the standings table, which additionally carries the 13 Week 10 game that has settled since this corpus was cut.
  • Bye arithmetic closes: 16 clubs show 8 games and the rest show 9, and the empty game-slots (16 per week × 9 weeks − 136 games = 8) times two clubs equals the 16 byes exactly.
  • 7 games are neutral-site/International Games; any home-field feature must exclude them, which is why the log carries the flag per row.

    What the schedule itself contributes as a feature

    Two of these games are structural exceptions a model must not treat as ordinary, and both are verifiable from the official schedule alone — no external dataset required:

    • NE at DET, 09:30 ET in Munich. A non-standard kickoff hour and an intercontinental trip for one club. We publish the fact, not a coefficient: the adjustment stays at zero until measured (SIG-10).
    • BAL Monday night, following a club that played Thursday the week prior. Rest asymmetry is computable from kickoff timestamps only, which makes it the cheapest defensible situational feature in the whole catalogue (SIG-09, S-16).

      The league's dated schedule announcements (BL-24)

      The live schedule above is a current-state table; the league also publishes dated announcements when it makes or changes a schedule. Both belong side by side, so a reader can hold a dated league statement against the current page and tell a transcription error from a genuine league move — the exact distinction FL-36 had to draw for two Week 11 broadcasts. The announcement register is published on the Results Feed page beside the verified results it accompanies: the Week 11 Schedule Change article of 9 November 2026, and the league's full 2026 schedule-release announcement. When an announcement and the live page disagree, this project publishes both dates and flags the delta; it never overwrites a transcription that was correct when it was made.
