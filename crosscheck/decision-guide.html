How to choose a path

A short guide to reviewing the suggestions: what to decide first, what can wait, and what happens if nothing is decided.

The order that avoids rework

  1. Decide the price question first (XQ-01). Every other decision about strategies, leaderboards and rewards is contingent on it. Deciding it last means rebuilding everything that assumed it.
  2. Decide the lineup authority second (XQ-02). It is cheap, it closes a requirement, and it does not depend on the price answer.
  3. Freeze the luck filters third (XQ-13, XQ-04). They must be written before results exist, or they will be written to fit whatever results arrive.
  4. Decide the reward question last (XQ-08). It is the only one where the answer is cosmetic until the first three are settled.

The consequence of deciding nothing

The repository will keep producing more design, in more trees, with more counts that disagree. That is the observed pattern across nine sessions. The cost of indecision is not a wrong number; it is an unreadable repository, and this build — a crosscheck table you are reading now — exists mostly to make that cost visible.

How to record a decision

Each suggestion and question has an id. Writing the id plus a word — adopt, reject, defer — in COORDINATION.md is enough for the next session to act on it. A decision that is not written down is re-litigated by every future session.
