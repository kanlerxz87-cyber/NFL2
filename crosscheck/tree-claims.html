The pattern in the results

Most claims are internally honest: a tree says twelve correlations and its own data file holds twelve. That is a real achievement given that these sessions could not see one another. The failures are of two kinds only, and both are cheap to fix:

  • Counts that drifted from the file they describe — three cases, all in prose rather than in data.
  • Claims that cannot be reproduced from the repository because their input is not committed — one case, the backtest that needs a price archive.

No case was found where a tree's data disagreed with the league page it cited. That is the headline: the evidence base is sounder than its prose.
