The arithmetic that gates every strategy

Nothing in this repository has produced a settled bet. Before one does, the reward rule has to know how wide a lucky streak can be. At the even-money break-even rate of 52.38%, the 95% band on realised ROI is:

A strategy that shows +5% ROI over 100 bets is entirely inside the band. The same +5% over 5,000 bets is not. Any leaderboard that does not show this number next to the ROI is ranking luck.

The recomputation table above is the counterpart: 6 coefficients, all reproduced to six decimal places from the committed game log. That is the standard the strategies should be held to.
