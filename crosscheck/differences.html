Why the added rows cluster in four clubs

The additions are not spread across the league. They concentrate in Seattle, Cincinnati, San Francisco, Indianapolis, Jacksonville and Arizona — and two of those clubs (Seattle and Cincinnati) are precisely the two whose blocks were empty on the page when the snapshot was taken. That correlation is the useful finding: an empty club block is not a healthy club, it is a filing that had not arrived. Any model that reads an empty block as zero availability risk would have been wrong six ways today.
