Methodology and limitations

What is verified, what is computed, and what the project refuses to infer.

Verification layers

  1. Structural: syntax, hashes, line counts, internal links, table shape and row-source coverage.
  2. Factual: official source actually read; stored value compared cell by cell; conflicts flagged.
  3. Derived: deterministic code over source-linked inputs; labelled as model output, not an NFL fact.
  4. Policy: gates and rewards are project decisions, not league rules or guarantees.

Never infer

Unconfirmable values are blank and flagged. Practice participation is not game status. Club depth ordering is not a confirmed lineup. Neutral-site games do not receive standard home-field adjustment. A source URL prefix is not proof of factual agreement.

Luck and validation

Luck cannot be eliminated. Controls include point-in-time availability, chronological walk-forward validation, locked holdout, cluster bootstrap intervals, Holm correction over the complete trial family, calibration, executable CLV/net ROI lower bounds, stability and paper drawdown caps. Minimum counts are necessary but never sufficient.

Settlement

Singles and parlays require explicit book-specific settlement. The engine tests pushes, voids and tie conventions; a void leg reduces the parlay price under the declared paper convention. Official NFL scoring rules do not establish sportsbook settlement.
