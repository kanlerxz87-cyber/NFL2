Executive summary

A decision-ready summary of verified facts, computed research and unresolved gaps.

Bottom line: the project now has a source-linked dashboard, a tested paper-only engine and an explicit review queue. It does not have a validated betting edge. The next decision is data access and pre-registration, not staking.

What is verified

Standings, schedule, injury listings and player/team statistics are inherited from official NFL.com transcriptions and reconciled by the repository verifiers. Q51 re-audited the original 48-file baseline line by line, recorded 18 findings, and rechecked the affected current files after upstream repairs. Two game-center pages were independently re-read as spot checks; the full 136-game log is reconciled to standings, not claimed as a fresh per-page re-transcription here.

What is computed

Thirteen Week 11 research lines use a predeclared ridge margin/total baseline on 136 completed games. Six descriptive Pearson correlations use official final scores. Intervals are withheld because only nine week clusters exist. These outputs are not calibrated probabilities, market edges or same-game prop correlations.

What remains blocked

Licensed opening/closing prices, prop thresholds, book-specific settlement contracts, play-by-play/snaps/routes, confirmed game-day lineups, weather and a locked out-of-sample holdout are missing. The collector is implemented and unit-tested but the live sandbox probe failed at the TLS boundary. No scheduled ingestion is claimed.

Competition and rewards

Separate boards are defined for singles, parlays and player-prop combos. Only gate-eligible paper strategies can rank; rewards are virtual badges. Monetary rewards, betting credits and real-money execution are disabled pending legal, funding and operational review.

Luck policy

Luck cannot be removed. The engine controls it with point-in-time guards, chronological validation, cluster bootstrap uncertainty, Holm multiplicity correction, calibration, executable CLV/net ROI lower bounds, stability checks and paper drawdown caps. A fixed bet count never proves skill.

Recommended next decision

Review the suggestions and questions. Choose a data-access path and pre-register one hypothesis family before collecting outcomes. Do not enable real-money execution.
