Parlay and prop-combo policy

Same-game legs require a trained joint distribution and book-specific settlement. Naive multiplication is prohibited. Cross-game independence must be tested by game/week clusters, not assumed. No prop thresholds or paired prop outcomes are present, so prop-leg coefficients remain blank.
