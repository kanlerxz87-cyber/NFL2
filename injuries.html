How to read NFL injury designations (official meanings)

Game statusOfficial meaningPractice codeMeaningSource
OUTWill not playDNPDid Not Participate in practiceNFL.com injuries
DoubtfulUnlikely to playLimitedLimited Participation in practiceNFL.com injuries
QuestionableUncertain whether the player will playFullFull Participation in practiceNFL.com injuries

“Probable” was retired in 2016; the current game-status scale is Out / Doubtful / Questionable only. Practice participation is reported separately from game status — a player can practice fully and still be Questionable, or sit out practice and still play. Always check both columns on the full report.

Depth-chart deficiency tracker (framework — fill from official depth charts)

Why this section is mostly blank: The NFL does not publish a single official “1st/2nd/3rd/4th string” depth chart per team on NFL.com. Teams publish their own depth charts, and beat reporters confirm starters. Until each team’s chart is captured with a link, the cells below stay blank and flagged — we will not guess who the backup is. See Flagged F-03 and Questions Q-07.

How to fill this tracker (one row per injured starter): (1) confirm starter + injury on the NFL.com report; (2) find the team’s official depth chart (team site) naming the next man up; (3) record games started / snaps for the replacement from an official stats source; (4) link every cell.
