A clean, read-only dashboard of verified NFL research, decision prerequisites, suggestions, strategy design, validation gates and injury/depth tracking. Every table row links to an official source. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are blank and flagged UNVERIFIED.

What this site is

A read-only research and decision dashboard. It does not place wagers, handle money or offer betting advice. Numerical results that cannot be confirmed from an official source are shown as blank cells with an UNVERIFIED flag.
