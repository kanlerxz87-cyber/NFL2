Competition design

Leaderboard & rewards

The competition design mirrors the NFL's official crowdsourced analytics competition: judged submissions, a documented prize pool, explicit PASS/FAIL standards and a public/private leaderboard split. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or Kaggle; those pages are cited as design references. No live-money feature is enabled.
