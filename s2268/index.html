What this session is. Every parallel session before this one had built and merged a large, verified dashboard at the repository root. What none of them had done was score anything: the leaderboard read “T0 — prospective, 0 of 14 gates” with every performance cell blank, and the one settled game had been graded by hand. This session does two things: it re-reads the official league pages on 13 November 2026 and publishes exactly what moved, and it builds the first pre-registered walk-forward competition so that the leaderboard has real numbers on it — including the numbers that say our own originated lines do not beat knowing nothing.

Read this first

Everything here is measured, or it is labelled not published. The competition is scored against the frozen weeks 1–9 corpus (136 games, every row carrying its own official nfl.com game page). It is not a betting record: no price feed exists anywhere in this repository, so there is no closing line, no vig, no CLV, no ROI and no units. Anywhere a price would be needed, the cell is blank and says so.

What moved on 13 November 2026

  • The official injury hub now publishes 295 Week 10 rows across 26 clubs. The page the site renders carries 254 rows across 24 clubs.
  • Against the published register there are 85 differences (rows added, removed or changed); against session a392a193’s later re-fetch there are 69, which is the same league page moving in about fourteen hours.
  • Three Week 10 game ids (W10-06, W10-07, W10-08) name different fixtures in data/injuries_2026_wk10.csv than they do in data/schedule_2026_wk10.csv. Any join between the two files attaches a club’s injuries to the wrong opponent.

What the competition found

  • Our originated spread does not beat a prediction of zero on the holdout (MAE 11.03 vs 10.92); the correlation between our predicted home margin and the actual margin is effectively nil.
  • Our originated total misses by 11.21 points against 11.18 for the do-nothing league mean. Also no improvement.
  • The best side selector wins 79% of its decisions, but it does so on 24 of them — below this build’s own 40-decision floor. Ten coin-flipping selectors produce a best-of-ten of 0.7083 or better one time in twenty by luck alone.
  • The strongest genuine signal is the boring one: pick the team with the better season point differential, 45 of 66. That is the definition of a favourite, not an edge, and without a price nobody can say whether it was already paid for. Its mirror image, the contrarian control, wins 21 of 66 on the same games.
