Interpreting the retrospective metrics

The favourite signal won 45 of 66 decisions, 68.2%, with an exact binomial p of 0.0043. This describes the historical sample, not an edge: a team with the better season point differential is the favourite, and winning two times in three is what being a favourite means. Whether that was already in the price is unknown. The contrarian control won 21 of 66 on the same games, the exact complement and a diagnostic for the test procedure.
