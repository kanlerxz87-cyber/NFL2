What the numbers say

  • Home team wins × home margin beats our spread: phi = 0.9449. They are almost the same bet. Our spread model barely moves off a constant, so “covers our number” and “wins the game” fire together. A two-leg parlay built from them is one leg sold twice, and the independence assumption would price it at 17.4% when it hits 41.7% of the time.
  • Home team wins × away team scores 24 or more: phi = −0.6400. Strongly negative, as it must be: the away team scoring heavily is evidence against the home team winning. Priced independently this pair would be badly wrong in the punter's favour, which is precisely why no book prices it that way.
  • Over our total × away team scores 24 or more: phi = 0.6112. Likewise positive and large.
  • Home team scores 24+ × away team scores 24+: phi = 0.0622. Nearly independent — the one pair here where the independence assumption does little damage.
