Week 11 venue register — transcribed from the official game centre

The brief asks for the venue of every fixture to be transcribed from the official game centre and published. It is now done for Week 11: all thirteen fixtures, read on 16 November 2026 at 18:40 UTC, one row per fixture, each row carrying that fixture's own official game page.

On this pageThe registerTwo official pages, three disagreementsWhat is deliberately not hereMethod

Before this page existed, the Week 11 schedule table carried not published in its venue column, because the only place the schedule page mentions a stadium is inside the destination parameter of its own hotel link. That reading was published as FL-112 and left as an owner decision. The game centre settles it: every game page prints a Game Info block with two labelled fields, Location and Stadium, and all thirteen print both.

Two official pages, three disagreements

The schedule page's hotel-link parameter and the game centre's Game Info block are both official league sources. They agree on nine fixtures and disagree on three:

W11-13 Bengals at CommandersNORTHWEST STADIUMFedEx FieldDifferent venue name

Two of the three are not sponsor-prefix differences. The game centre prints the name each venue stopped using, which matters for more than tidiness: any join from this register to a weather, roof or surface dataset keyed on the current sponsored name will silently fail on those two rows. Both readings are published, the disagreement is FL-115, and which one this project treats as its system of record is an owner decision.

The neutral-site fixture is the one the register improves rather than argues about. The schedule page named only "Mexico City"; the game centre for W11-12 prints Location MEXICO CITY, Stadium ESTADIO BANORTE.

What is deliberately not here

  • No roof class and no playing surface. The brief's stadium roof and surface field (BL-59) is still
  • open. The game centre publishes neither, and no league-operated endpoint that does was reachable from
  • this session. Those two columns stay blank rather than being filled from a club marketing page or a
  • third-party stadium database, because a wrong roof class misprices a total in a specific direction and
  • a blank one misprices nothing. What is now in place is the join key: a per-fixture official venue name
  • and location, so the day an official roof or surface source is approved, it has something to attach to.
  • No weather values. The information-gaps register carries the forecast
  • question. Nothing is published until a forecast source is approved and captured with a timestamp.
  • No Week 10 venues from this source. Week 10's venues came from the official injury-report article
  • and are carried at their own provenance on the schedule page. They have not been
  • re-transcribed here, so this register is a Week 11 artefact, not a claim about the season.

    • Method

    1. Each of the thirteen Week 11 game-centre pages was fetched and its Game Info block read. Location and
    2. Stadium were copied verbatim, including the letter case the page uses.
    3. tools/build_0a28b225.py joins those transcriptions to data/schedule_week11_2026.csv by game id and
    4. writes data/venue_register_0a28b225.csv.
    5. tools/verify_0a28b225.py re-derives the file from the inputs: one row per fixture, every game id real,
    6. away/home/date/kickoff equal to the schedule file's own values, every row citing its own
    7. nfl.com/games/ page, no blank stadium cell, exactly three conflicts, and the neutral fixture the one
    8. the schedule marks neutral. All 30 checks pass.

      9. Sources: Week 11 game centres (S-WK11-GC-0A28) and the Week 11 schedule page for the parameter reading recorded under FL-112.
