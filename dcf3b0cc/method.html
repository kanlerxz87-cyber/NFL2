Method

How the page was read

The official hub at nfl.com/injuries was fetched in eight sequential reads beginning at 2026-11-15T12:54Z and completed at 2026-11-15T12:55Z. Every club table was transcribed row by row as the page printed it: player, position, injury, practice status, game status, and the player page the row links to. Nothing was normalised except for joining purposes, and no value was inferred.

What was compared to what

  • data/nfl_injury_hub_20261115_dcf3b0cc.csv — today's transcription, 347 rows.
  • data/injuries_wk10_83246f00.csv — the repository's published Week 10 table, 296 rows, read 2026-11-13T12:46Z from the same URL.
  • data/s745_depth_charts.csv — 1,558 club depth-chart rows transcribed 2026-11-13 by an earlier session, each carrying its own club URL.
  • data/standings_2026.csv — nfl.com standings, 32 clubs, captured 2026-11-13T12:53Z.

Limits stated up front

  • Rank order in a club depth chart is not playing time, and clubs label their own charts unofficial.
  • Designations are dated 2026-11-15 and charts 2026-11-13; the join spans two days.
  • "Not listed on the report" is not the same as "available": players on injured reserve do not appear on the weekly report at all, and no reserve list is captured in this session.
  • Correlations are cross-sectional over one week and are not predictive.
