Executive Summary

Snapshot 2026-11-12 · 2026 REG season · All factual claims below link to their official source. Analysis is labelled as analysis.

1 · What this project is

NFL2 collects, organizes, and analyzes NFL data from official sources to support better decisions, and builds toward originating our own fair lines with a skill-based strategy leaderboard (single bets, parlays, player props) where luck is systematically removed via validation gates, correlation accounting, and closing-line value (CLV). This page summarizes what is verified today, what is missing, and what must be decided before any line or strategy goes live.
