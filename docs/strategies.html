Correlation — what it is and what we claim (and don’t)

For parlays/SGPs, legs are not independent. If QB passing yards go over, his WR’s receiving over is more likely (positive correlation). Books price SGPs on joint probability P(A∩B), not P(A)·P(B) — positively-correlated combos pay less than naive multiplication. Sources: NFL Player Bets — SGP Strategy · Parlay Betting Explained (correlation discount) · Player Props Guide (SGP margin).

Removing luck — the skill stack

  • CLV (closing-line value): Beat the closing price consistently = market agrees you found value. Primary metric because it is least luck-contaminated.
  • Calibration (log-loss/Brier): Do your probabilities match reality out-of-sample? Beats ROI for small samples.
  • Sample size + CIs: 500+ bets, bootstrap CIs on every metric. A 60% win rate in 50 bets is noise; in 500 bets it is signal.
  • Pre-registration: No cherry-picking. Hypothesis first, bets second.
  • Staking discipline: Fractional Kelly (see calculator). Full Kelly on uncertain edges is ruin math, not skill.
