How to read this page (and what it cannot do)

  • Practice trend > label: DNP→DNP→Limited with Questionable usually plays (snap-count risk); DNP all week usually sits. Final word is the 90-minutes-before-kickoff inactive list.
  • IR/PUP/NFI ≠ game status: Those are roster mechanisms (off the 53-man roster), not Questionable/Doubtful/Out. DTR = Designated to Return (21-day window).
  • What’s missing: Full 200+ player list, Thu/Fri updates, game-status designations, inactives, snap-count implications. All pending Q-01. Raw data: data/injuries_2026-11-11.json.
