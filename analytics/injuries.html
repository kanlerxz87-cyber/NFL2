Injuries & depth

Who is hurt — and whether they actually start

The official injury report tells you a player is injured and how much he practised. It does not tell you whether he is a starter. Joining it to each club's own published depth chart answers that — for the four clubs whose charts were transcribed and verified. For the rest, the answer is left visibly blank. This is the difference between a real deficiency analysis and a plausible-looking one.

The limitation, stated up front. Every club labels its depth chart unofficial. It is the organisation's own working document, not a league-mandated designation, and it can lag a coaching decision. Charts were transcribed and cross-checked for Kansas City, Green Bay, New England and Detroit only. Transcribing the other 28 and publishing them unverified would break the no-inference rule, so they are blank.
