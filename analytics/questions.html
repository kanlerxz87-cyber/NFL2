Q-16How large is a roster, and how many players can actually play on game day?
53 on the active/inactive list, and 47 or 48 on the game-day roster — 48 only if at least eight offensive linemen are active, otherwise 47. Practice-squad elevation can lift the active list to 54 or 55.

Q-08Is there a home-field advantage in the 2026 data?
Not detectable at this sample size, and the naive figure is misleading. Pooling all 136 games gives a mean home margin of +0.00. But 7 games were at neutral sites with no true home club. On the 129 genuine home games the mean home margin is +0.50 points, 95% CI [-1.80, +2.79]. The conventional ~+2.5 prior sits inside that interval, so 2026 cannot reject it. The model uses +0.50.
PARTIALsource ↗Close it with P-05: recompute the same split over 2023-2025 to see whether 2026 is an artefact of one partial season.IR-04. Neutral-site games are excluded from the estimate and disclosed, not silently pooled. See `analysis.json → home_field`.

Q-10Do any of the six strategies clear the validation gates?
None. All six are UNDERPOWERED — the walk-forward backtest grades 15-72 legs per strategy against a gate that requires 200. S1, S2 and S3 pass four of the seven gates but cannot be certified on sample size.
PARTIALsource ↗Keep appending weeks; G2 needs roughly three further seasons of the same walk-forward to clear organically. Widening the fold window is the alternative and is proposed as proposal P-03.Gate table on the Strategies page; G2 (n ≥ 200) fails for every strategy.
Q-11Which club metrics actually correlate with winning?
Net rating (r = +0.876), power (r = +0.876), Pythagorean residual (r = +0.805), offensive rating (r = +0.694) and defensive rating (r = +0.666) all correlate strongly with win percentage across 32 clubs. The strongest single driver is net yards per play.
ANSWEREDsource ↗—Correlation table on the Analysis page, n = 32.
Q-12Are game totals and margins independent of each other?
Yes, effectively. Total points vs margin correlates at −0.161, total points vs absolute margin at −0.016, and the two clubs' scores within a game correlate at only +0.076. That is the empirical justification for treating 'who wins' and 'how high' as separate research problems.
ANSWEREDsource ↗—Game-level correlation table, n = 136.
Q-23Is the line sign convention correct, or is the model upside down?
It is checked, not assumed. A power-rating line picks the home side correctly 64.6% of the time; an inverted sign would score below 50%. The build asserts this and aborts if it ever inverts.
ANSWEREDsource ↗—`analysis.json → sign_check`.

Q-15How were the 8 published passing lines proven?
By equality. A passer is published only when all 15 of his values — attempts, completions, yards, touchdown passes, interceptions, completion percentage, yards per attempt, sacks, sack yards and the rest — are identical to his club's official team passing line, which can only happen if he is the club's sole passer. Detroit's Jared Goff fails this test (2,134 vs a Detroit team figure of 2,145) and is therefore excluded, not reconciled.
ANSWEREDsource ↗—Rule stated on the Data Sources page; 8 passers pass, Goff is the counter-example that establishes the rule.
