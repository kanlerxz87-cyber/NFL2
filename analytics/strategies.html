Strategies

A leaderboard that is designed to reject its own entries

Six strategies were specified, implemented and backtested walk-forward across weeks 5–9: for every graded game the ratings use only games played before it. Seven gates decide whether a strategy has earned the right to be called an edge. None of the six currently passes all seven, and the site says so before it shows any hit rate.

Result: 0 of 6 validated. Every strategy is UNDERPOWERED — the largest graded sample is 72 legs against a gate that requires 200. This is not a failure of the code; it is the honest state of a single partial season. The leaderboard is sorted by gates passed, not by hit rate, precisely so that a reader cannot mistake the top row for a recommendation.

The seven gates

A strategy must clear all seven. Six are hard; passing fewer than six is a rejection regardless of results.

Independent hit probability

Even four legs at an optimistic 60% each is barely a one-in-eight proposition. That is not a reason to avoid parlays; it is a reason to be extremely careful about claiming edge on them.

Measured over 136 games: r(home score, away score) = +0.076. That means the two sides of a game are close to independent, so multiplying the leg probabilities is a defensible approximation for THIS season - but the estimate is weak and is not a licence to assume independence in general. The correlation is estimated on a single partial season, so it is reported with its n and treated as provisional rather than as a structural law.

Player-prop combinations cannot be measured at all. UNMEASURABLE from official sources in this environment: per-game, per-player box scores are not available (only season aggregates), so the correlation between a player's yardage and his club's team total cannot be quantified. See Flagged for Review FL-09.

No market, therefore no ROI. No NFL.com property publishes a spread, total, moneyline or prop price — verified rather than assumed. So this page reports hit rate, Brier skill and calibration, and refuses to report a return, a profit, or a closing-line value. Any project that shows you an ROI without an odds source is guessing. This is FL-01.
