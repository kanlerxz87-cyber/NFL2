Originating your own lines

Strategies & signals

NFL.com does not publish its own betting lines: its widgets show odds, spreads and totals supplied by official bookmaker partners. 'Originating my own lines' is therefore defined as computing an independent pre-game probability/line from official league data and comparing it with a licensed market line. A difference is a candidate signal, not an edge.
