Depth Cascades — Re-verified Against the Official Pages

The brief asks the site to know when a defence or an offence is down to its second, third or fourth string. The earlier depth register (18 cascades, Week 10) made that claim with labels such as *Names and DNP Verified*. On 13 November 2026 each cascade was checked, player by player, against the club's own depth chart and the league's own Week 10 injury report. This page publishes the outcome, including every contradiction.

What was checked, and against what

Two sources, both read on 13 November 2026:

  1. The club's depth chart for each of the 12 clubs named in the cascades. Each chart was read in full, every page in one piece, on the club's own website. Several clubs label their chart unofficial, and the Patriots' page is titled "Unofficial Depth Chart". The label on each page is recorded in FL-50; it is not assumed.
  2. The official NFL Week 10 injury report at nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/REG10, read in all seven pages. Players placed on injured reserve are not on the weekly report; their status is taken from NFL.com news articles and marked NEWS_ONLY (FL-52).

    3. Nothing is inferred. A player who does not appear on the report is shown as not on report, which is a fact about the report and not a statement that he is healthy.

    Verdicts

    VerdictMeaning
    VERIFIEDEvery named player is placed where the cascade says on the club's chart, and every status the cascade gives matches the report. Players the cascade says are absent from the chart are counted as consistent.
    PARTLYThe core string is confirmed, but at least one status is wrong, one injury detail is not on the official injury report table, or a slot is contested because the chart lists one slot per row.
    CONTRADICTEDThe chart order or the status of a named starter disagrees with the cascade. The cascade should not be used until it is corrected.
