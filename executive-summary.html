3. What the analysis layer shows (computed from official results, never market-priced)

  • 136-game walk-forward ratings study (weeks 1–9 official results): originated spread MAE 9.278 vs 10.455 do-nothing baseline; paired against the archived third-party price +0.295 pts with a bootstrap CI spanning zero — no provable edge, none claimed. Originated totals MAE 11.165 vs 11.078 league mean — worse than do-nothing, published as diagnostic only (S-37).
  • Home field on 2026 games: +0.50 pts, 95% CI [−1.80, +2.80]. The customary +2.5 prior sits outside the interval, but 149 games cannot settle it — the model prints +0.50 with its interval wherever it appears.
  • Correlations, computed only from officially sourced inputs with n and interval beside each value: same-game holdout pairings up to φ = 0.945 (one bet sold twice); prop-level correlations are blank — no prop-line archive exists (Q-12).
  • Leaderboard: empty by design. Zero strategies carry stakes; ranking is proper-score (Brier skill with a game-cluster lower bound and multiplicity adjustment); control baselines (random picks, favorites only) are scored in every run; the best raw hit rate (79.2% on 24 decisions) is published in full and deliberately not promoted. Signal and staking are separate columns; staking allocation exists as a column family but carries no allocation until G-14.
  • Pre-registration: hash-stamped ledger; the Week 11 fair lines were hashed before kickoff and the grader recomputes every hash before grading — tested, a one-cell edit reports BROKEN and exits 1. The forward grade stays empty until games settle.
