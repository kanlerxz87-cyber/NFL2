Lines Lab — Originate Your Own Lines

A transparent, reproducible way to turn verified team data into your own fair spread, total, and moneyline — so you can compare any market line against your number instead of guessing. Everything here is a model estimate, not a sportsbook line.

Model only: Outputs below are computed live in your browser from the numbers you enter. They are not odds from any sportsbook, not predictions to bet on, and not financial advice. Never stake money on a model output without independent verification and a bankroll plan. See validation gates before trusting any signal.

1 · The math (baseline v0 — deliberately simple)

Start from points for/against (verified on Standings). For a home/away pair with g games each:

avgFor = PF / g avgAgainst = PA / g projHome = (homeAvgFor + awayAvgAgainst) / 2 + HFA projAway = (awayAvgFor + homeAvgAgainst) / 2 fairSpread (home) = projHome − projAway (positive = home favoured) fairTotal = projHome + projAway P(home win) ≈ Φ( spread / SD ) (Φ = normal CDF; SD ≈ 13.5 pts typical margin SD) fair ML from P: favourite −100·P/(1−P) · underdog +100·(1−P)/P

HFA (home-field advantage) defaults to 2.5 pts and is editable — it is a model assumption, not an NFL-published constant. The normal approximation ignores key numbers (3 and 7) and push probabilities; v1 should use an empirical margin distribution. Scoring values used for context: touchdown 6, field goal 3, safety 2, try 1 or 2 — NFL Official Playing Rules 2026.
