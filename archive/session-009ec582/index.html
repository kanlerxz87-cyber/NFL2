An NFL analytics build where every number can be traced to an official page

This site does three things: it reproduces the league's own published records from a 136-game log and proves the reproduction arithmetically; it researches betting-shaped ideas under a leaderboard whose validation gates it is not allowed to pass by storytelling; and it tracks injuries against club depth charts so that a missing star can be told apart from a missing fourth-stringer. Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, the cell is blank and the item is on the Flagged for Review page. No figure here is invented, and no strategy is claimed to work.

Four things worth knowing

Home-field advantage is not detectable in 2026 — and the naive number is wrong

Pooled across all 136 games the mean home margin is +0.00 points, which looks like home-field has vanished. But 7 of those games were at neutral sites with no real home club. On the 129 genuine home games the mean is +0.50 points, 95% CI [-1.80, +2.79]. The conventional ~+2.5 prior sits inside that interval, so this season cannot reject it. The model uses +0.50. Method →

Team stats conserve exactly — which is why the mapping can be trusted

League-wide offence equals league-wide defence on eight separate identities: 63,556 passing yards, 30,580 rushing yards, 641 sacks, 411 passing TD, 257 rushing TD, 197 interceptions, 3,006 passing first downs, 1,757 rushing first downs. Exact conservation is what proved the header-less columns were mapped correctly. Team stats →

Starter vs backup is answered for 4 clubs, blank for 28

The official injury report says who is hurt but never says whether they start. Joining it to club-published depth charts resolves that for Kansas City, Green Bay, New England and Detroit — and the join surfaces 12 injured players who are absent from their own club's chart entirely. For the other 28 clubs the field is left blank rather than guessed. Injuries & depth →

Two leaderboards are withheld because their columns cannot be proven

The rushing and receiving leaderboards render with no header row, and no arithmetic identity identifies the columns — published yards-per-carry disagrees with yards divided by carries for every sampled row. They are not published at all. Only 8 passing lines survive an exact equality test against their club's official team passing line. Flagged for Review →
