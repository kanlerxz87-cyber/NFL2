2 · The system we are building

  • Verified data layer — standings, schedule, injuries transcribed from NFL.com with per-row source links (Standings, Schedule, Injuries). Anything unverified stays blank and flagged.
  • Lines Lab — originate your own fair spread/total/moneyline from verified inputs with fully shown math (Lines Lab). Baseline v0 is live; opponent-adjusted v1+ is the next build.
  • Strategy Leaderboard — single bets, parlays, and prop combos compete under validation gates G1–G7 (pre-registration, sample size, out-of-sample, CLV, ROI with CI, correlation, stability) so luck cannot rank (Leaderboard). No results claimed yet.
  • Injury & depth tracker — starter-out → replacement → deficiency signal, filled only from official reports + team depth charts (Injuries). Depth charts are the current bottleneck (F-03).

3 · What we do NOT know yet (decision blockers)

  • Play-by-play / EPA data — needed for v2 ratings and real backtests (Q-04, Q-05).
  • Historical odds (open + close) — needed for CLV and ROI; without them the leaderboard cannot run (Q-05).
  • Official depth charts — needed to quantify backup deficiencies (Q-07, F-03).
  • Market-line capture method — which licensed books, timestamps, and storage for “market vs your fair” comparisons (Q-06).
