    Method

    1. Every official page was fetched and read in this session on 16 November 2026; the fetch window is 14:35–15:05Z. The hub read is transcribed row by row to data/hub_read_e632af20_20261116.csv with its capture clock on every row, the standings read to data/standings_recheck_e632af20_20261116.csv.
    2. tools/verify_e632af20.py re-derives every arithmetic claim on this page from the checked-in files: the zero-change hub delta, the standings reconciliation club by club, the league-wide identities, the result scores against the fixture map and the results file, and the Week 11 fixture facts against the schedule file. It exits non-zero if any claim moves.
    3. The verification suite that builds and audits the whole site (tools/build_site.py, tools/verify.py, tools/check_site.py, scripts/audit_all_pages.py and the other session suites) was re-run on the combined tree; the tally is recorded in COORDINATION.md.

      4. What this page does not establish

      • It does not grade anything. The settled table still grades one game; extending it is blocked on the append protocol in FL-112, not on missing evidence — every result it needs is in the table above.
      • It does not refresh the site's cumulative tables. The standings, ratings and fair-line tables keep their dated snapshots until Q-40 sets a cadence; this page publishes the delta beside them instead.
      • It is transcription, not byte capture. The rows were transcribed from the rendered pages and then verified by the arithmetic above and by cross-reading two official pages per result. A raw capture with stored hashes is still the missing infrastructure (FL-82).
