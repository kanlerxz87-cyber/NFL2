Why a bounds register exists

A decision made on an unbounded estimate is a guess wearing a suit. This register states every number this dashboard uses, the evidence under it, the limit of that evidence, and the rule that governs how it may be published. Nothing in the table is a market line, and nothing in it is a forecast presented as fact.

The rule the bounds impose

  • If an estimate's interval spans zero, the site prints the interval, not the point estimate.
  • If the evidence is one season, the site says "one season" on the same line as the number.
  • If a value could only come from a source we do not have, the cell stays blank.

That is why the line lab publishes home-field advantage as zero rather than the +0.50 measured this season: a one-season estimate with a wide interval is not a parameter, and pretending otherwise would make every line look more precise than the evidence supports.
