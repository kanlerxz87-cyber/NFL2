The P0 questions behind the flags

- **Q-101 Price feed** — Which official or licensed source supplies opening and closing prices, at what latency, and under what terms? - **Q-102 Settlement** — What is the official settlement rule set for pushes, ties, half-point lines and void legs? - **Q-103 Player-level data** — Are we entitled to store and re-price snap counts, routes, targets and participation? - **Q-104 Depth authority** — What is authoritative for depth order when every club chart is explicitly unofficial? - **Q-108 Sample size** — How many graded bets does a strategy need before its record is evidence rather than variance? - **Q-109 Correlation inputs** — What joint-outcome data is required to estimate parlay and prop-combo correlation? - **Q-110 Reward design** — What does the leaderboard pay, to whom, and on what schedule? - **Q-111 Luck definition** — Which luck filters are mandatory before a strategy may be called skill-based?

These are the only items where a decision from the owner, rather than more work from a session, changes the answer. They are also the items most likely to be mistaken for research tasks, which is why they are repeated here.
