What this dashboard is

A second, additive dashboard inside the NFL2 repository. It does not restate the numbers published by the other trees; it re-derives the ones it can and links to the rest. Its job is to answer three questions honestly: what do we actually know, what is missing, and what would we build first.

The whole build follows one rule: a number with no official source is not printed. Where a value cannot be confirmed, the cell says not published and the row says why.

The five findings that matter

  1. The standings table is exact. All 32 clubs, 7 shared fields, 224 cells: zero mismatches against

the official league table, and points-for equals points-against at 6,246.

  1. The injury table is stale and incomplete. The official hub now carries 277 practice-participation

rows across 26 clubs; the stored table has 254 rows across 24 clubs, with two clubs missing entirely. Five players disagree on practice status and two stored rows are no longer on the official page.

  1. No market data exists. Not one price, in any file. Every expected-value, closing-line and vig

statement in the repository is therefore unverifiable, and this dashboard leaves those cells blank.

  1. Our model is not yet better than guessing. Leave-one-week-out over 136 games: spread MAE 10.55

against a zero-line baseline of 10.62 (difference interval includes zero). Total MAE is worse than the league average: 12.86 vs 11.62.

  1. Depth charts are unofficial by their own labels. Two of the three club charts inspected say so in

as many words. Three clubs are covered; twenty-nine are not.
