What this page covers, and what it does not

Injury participation for 26 clubs as published on the official hub at fetch time, and club depth charts for 3 of 32 clubs, transcribed in full. The shortfall is stated on the page rather than implied away: 29 clubs have no depth chart here, and every league-wide claim on this page is scoped to the three clubs that do.

Two of the three charts are described as unofficial by the club that publishes them. That is not a technicality; it means a depth chart cannot be cited as an official source, and the only defensible depth authority is realised playing time, which requires participation data we do not have (Q-103).

A cascade means the starter was out or did not practise AND the next man up was also out or did not practise, so the club goes to its third name.

The one genuine cascade

New England's running-back line is the only true cascade at these three clubs. The starter, Rhamondre Stevenson, did not practise; the next man up, TreVeyon Henderson, was ruled out; the first available body is the third name on the chart, Corey Kiner. That is exactly the "down to a third-stringer" case the requirement is about.

Every other affected line has a healthy next man up. That is a finding too: at these three clubs, on this filing, depth is mostly holding.

The trap on this page

"Did not practise" is not "will not play", and a blank game-status cell is not "probable" — it is "not yet filed". This page therefore carries three states, never two, and never converts a practice status into a prediction of availability.
