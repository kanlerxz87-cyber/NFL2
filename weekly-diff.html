Two dated, independent transcriptions of the same official standings page already exist in this repository — 13 November 2026 (S-STAND-653B) and 16 November 2026 (S-STAND-F3EC) — so the change across the Week 10 window can be measured rather than asserted. Thirty-two rows below, one per club.

Read the window before you read the numbers

The two reads are dated, so the window between them is a date window, not a week number. That matters, and it is the one thing on this page worth understanding:

  • The Thursday game is outside it. Commanders at Giants was played on 12 November, before the first
  • read. Washington and the Giants therefore show zero games played in the window even though both
  • played in Week 10.
  • The Monday game is outside it. Chargers at Ravens kicks at 20:15 ET on 16 November, after the
  • second read. Both clubs also show zero, and the fixture-context register marks
  • that game scheduled, not final at read time.
  • So the window holds 12 games, not 13. The verifier proves it: total games played across the 32 rows
  • is 24 team-games, which is exactly twice the twelve Week 10 results dated between the two reads.

    • Eight clubs show zero games: the four on a bye (Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia, Tampa Bay) plus the four above whose Week 10 game fell outside the window. A diff that cannot say which zero is which is a diff that will mislead someone, so the page says it.

    What this page is not

    • It is not a full Week 10 diff. It is the change between two dated reads. The two games outside the
    • window are named above rather than folded in, because folding them in would mean inventing a read that
    • does not exist.
    • It is not a rating change. The brief's diff column asks for rank, rating and availability change.
    • Rank is here. Rating change is not: no dated rating snapshot pair exists, so it stays blank. Availability
    • change is not here either — that lives on the injury lineage and
    • hub delta pages, which diff dated reads of a different page.
    • It is not a stored series yet. These are two reads that happened to be taken. A weekly snapshot
    • store with retained hashes is BL-29/BL-30/BL-48 and remains not started; until it exists, a "weekly" diff
    • can only be produced when two reads happen to be on file.

      • Method

      1. tools/build_0a28b225.py reads data/standings_recheck_20261113.csv and data/standings_2026.csv,
      2. computes each club's win/loss/tie, points, net and within-division rank on each read, differences them,
      3. and writes data/weekly_diff_0a28b225.csv.
      4. tools/verify_0a28b225.py re-derives all 32 rows from the two reads — every level, every delta, both
      5. ranks and the rank change — then proves the window: total games played must equal twice the number of
      6. Week 10 results whose date falls strictly between the two read dates, and the zero-game clubs must be
      7. exactly the clubs absent from those results. 38 checks pass.
      8. Reproduce with python3 tools/build_0a28b225.py && python3 tools/verify_0a28b225.py.

        9. Sources: both dated reads of the official standings page — S-STAND-653B (2026-11-13T12:53Z) and S-STAND-F3EC (2026-11-16T14:57Z) — cited on every row.
